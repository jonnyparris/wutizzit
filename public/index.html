      How to play

      • 🎨 Create a room and share the link
      • ✏️ Draw your word when it's your turn
      • 🤔 Guess other players' drawings in the chat
      • 🏆 Faster guesses score more points

      Features

      • ⚡ Real-time multiplayer on Cloudflare's network
      • 🔁 Rejoin after a refresh without losing your score
      • 💡 Letter hints and "so close!" feedback
      • 🎯 Custom word lists, pause and owner controls

      About

      Wutizzit is a drawing and guessing game for friends and family. Made with ❤️ for creative minds.
